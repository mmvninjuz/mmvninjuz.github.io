Ninja Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This is the privacy policy for the Ninja apps: Ninja (music) and Ninja Video. They are made by Puna Pty Ltd, Australia (“we”). To ask us a privacy question, use the email under Contact at the end of this page.

The short version

  • Ninja listens for the word “Ninja” through your microphone. That listening is worked out on your phone, offline.
  • We have no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics. We do not sell your information.
  • The apps do not send anything to us automatically.
  • Ninja Video can play a YouTube video you share to it, so you can change its speed. When you use that, information goes to YouTube and Google. See “YouTube” below.
  • Ninja is for people aged 13 and over.

Ninja (music) does not use the internet.

Your voice

Listening for “Ninja” is done by a speech recogniser inside the app, on your phone, offline. Ninja listens while it is turned on, and a notification shows when it is listening. Say “Ninja off” to stop it.

For some commands, such as finding a video by name, Ninja Video uses your phone’s own speech service (part of Android). Ninja asks it to work offline, but that is only a request. Depending on the speech service on your phone, your voice for that command may be sent to the service’s provider, which is often Google. That is controlled by your phone and its speech service, under their privacy policies, not by Ninja.

Ninja speaks to you using your phone’s text-to-speech engine. Which engine it is, and whether it uses the internet, is a setting on your phone.

YouTube

Ninja Video uses YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube features you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s use of information is explained in the Google Privacy Policy.

Ninja does not search YouTube. You find videos in the YouTube app. When you share a video to Ninja:

  • The video’s YouTube id is sent to YouTube to check whether the video is marked “Made for Kids”.
  • YouTube and Google can see your phone’s internet address.
  • Ninja keeps the video’s link and speed only while it plays. Ninja does not keep a list of the videos you watch.

While a video plays in the YouTube app, Ninja can pause it, skip it and change its volume if you turn on notification access. Ninja only sends the YouTube app the control you asked for. It reads the title, channel and position on your phone only.

When you play a video, it plays in YouTube’s own player, inside Ninja Video. That player belongs to YouTube. It may collect information, and may use cookies or similar technology on your phone, as set out in Google’s privacy policy. YouTube may also show its own ads in videos. Ninja does not control or block them.

Ninja Video does not play videos that YouTube marks as “Made for Kids”.

Ninja does not ask you to sign in to a Google account and does not receive your Google account information.

Web sites

Ninja Video can open web sites that you choose, in its own web view, so you can control the video on the page. The site you open sees your internet address and may set cookies, as it would in any browser. Those cookies are kept inside Ninja Video on your phone. If you type a password into a site, that goes to the site; Ninja does not read what you type. Ninja does not choose or send you to any site by itself.

What is saved on your phone

  • Voice setup recordings. During voice setup the app saves short recordings of you saying the commands. They stay in the app’s private storage on your phone, are not copied to cloud backup, and are not sent to us.
  • Settings. For example whether setup is finished, whether Ninja is turned off, and whether you agreed to the YouTube terms and this policy.
  • Your recordings. Videos you record are saved to your phone’s gallery or Download folder. They are yours. The apps do not upload them.
  • What you say. Ninja does not keep a log of the words it hears.

Who we share information with

We do not receive your information, so we do not share it. The apps have no advertising, analytics or crash reporting. The only outside parties involved are the ones built into your phone (the speech recogniser and text-to-speech engine), YouTube and Google when you use the YouTube features, and web sites you choose to open.

Problem reports

If something goes wrong you can choose to email us about it. We only receive what you choose to send.

Children

Ninja is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The apps do not ask your age. If you think a child under 13 has used the apps, contact us.

How long information is kept, and how to delete it

  • Everything in the app’s private storage (setup recordings, settings, web site cookies) is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings.
  • Videos and recordings you made are in your gallery or Download folder and stay after you uninstall. Delete them in your Gallery or Files app whenever you like.
  • We hold none of your information, so there is nothing on our side to delete. If you emailed us a report, ask us and we will delete it.
  • To stop the microphone, say “Ninja off”, or switch the microphone off for the app in Android Settings, Apps, Permissions.

Security

Listening and recognition happen on your phone. The app’s private files are in Android’s app storage, which other apps cannot read. Files in your gallery or Download folder can be read by you and by apps you allow to see your files. Requests Ninja sends to YouTube use an encrypted (https) connection.

Your choices

You can refuse any permission, switch one off later in Android Settings, say “Ninja off”, turn off notification access, or uninstall the apps. Without the microphone, Ninja cannot work.
