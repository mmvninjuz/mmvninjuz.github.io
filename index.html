Ninja

Ninja apps are made by Puna Pty Ltd, Australia. They are voice-control apps for Android phones. You say “Ninja” and then what you want.

Ninja (music)

Plays music stored on your phone and lets you control it by voice.

Ninja Video

Controls videos by voice, records video with your voice, and can play a YouTube video you share to it at the speed you ask for.
